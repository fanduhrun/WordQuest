About these options

Question count is capped by the words available in your selected lesson and filters. Scored Tests track graded attempts, correct answers, misses, and mistaken choices. Practice uses the same question engine and Coach Mode, earns effort rewards, and does not change mastery or scored Progress. Test emphasizes recognition and context; Challenge adds application; God Mode adds advanced application and relationships. “Needs practice” uses actual misses; “Not yet tested” means zero graded attempts.

⚡ Challenge Mode

Uses context, fill-in-the-blank, antonyms, reverse meanings, and closer distractors.

Challenge: harder questions, no analogies. God Mode: adds explicit synonym/antonym analogies and the toughest distractors.
